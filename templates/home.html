How the detector works

The current application preprocesses the submitted text, converts it into a TF-IDF feature vector, and sends that vector to the trained classifier.

01

Text preprocessing

The submitted article is cleaned, converted to lowercase, tokenized, stemmed and filtered using English stopwords.

02

TF-IDF representation

The processed text is transformed into the feature representation expected by the existing trained model.

03

ML classification

The trained classifier produces the application's current FAKE or REAL prediction.
